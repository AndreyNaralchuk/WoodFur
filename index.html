The Problem

The hidden cost of "fake" wood furniture.

Most furniture sold today isn't real wood — it's a chemical cocktail disguised as nature.

01

The Toxic Air

You buy a sleek, "wood-look" desk for your child.

The consequence: The particleboard core off-gasses urea-formaldehyde and chemical glues for years, giving them morning headaches and ruining your indoor air quality.

02

The Peeling Veneer

A fork scratches your new dining table, or a heavy backpack scuffs the desk.

The consequence: The thin paper veneer tears, exposing the ugly, moisture-swollen cardboard core underneath, forcing you to throw it away in 18 months.

03

The Hollow Feel

You run your hand across your modern furniture.

The consequence: It feels cold, hollow, and plasticky, making your home feel like a sterile showroom instead of a warm, natural sanctuary.

Our Solution

We build pure wood furniture that solves this.

🌿

Breathe Clean, Natural Air

We mill our furniture from kiln-dried solid oak with zero added chemical glues → your family breathes clean, natural air and avoids toxic off-gassing.

🪵

Built to Last 50+ Years

We use 1.5-inch thick solid timber sanded all the way through the core → you get a surface that can be re-sanded and refinished, lasting 50+ years instead of 18 months.

✋

Warm, Living Touch

We finish every piece with hand-rubbed natural beeswax and linseed oil → you get furniture that feels warm to the touch and develops a rich, unique patina over time.
